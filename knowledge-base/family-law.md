# Family law

<!-- Sample content for a fictional firm. General information only, not legal advice. -->

## Divorce

We help with contested and uncontested divorces, division of property and debts, and spousal support questions. We aim for respectful, practical solutions and use negotiation or mediation where it makes sense.

## Child custody

We help parents prepare parenting plans, negotiate custody and visitation (parenting time) arrangements, and request changes when circumstances change. Courts generally focus on the best interests of the child. Learn more on the Child Custody page: /practice-areas/child-custody.

## Child support

Child support is generally calculated using state guidelines that consider income and parenting time. We help with establishing support, reviewing existing orders and requesting modifications after significant changes, such as a change in income or custody. Learn more on the Child Support page: /practice-areas/child-support.

## Mediation

Mediation is a process where a neutral mediator helps both sides reach an agreement without a trial. Desmond Pea is trained in mediation and can explain whether it may suit your situation during a consultation.

## Safety

If you or your children are in danger, call 911 first. Once you are safe, we can talk with you about protective orders and next steps.
