# About Amber & Pea Law

<!-- Sample content: Amber & Pea Law is a fictional firm created for a portfolio project. -->

## Who we are

Amber & Pea Law is a small law firm that focuses on two areas: personal injury and family law. The firm was founded by Amber Calloway and Desmond Pea with the goal of making the legal process understandable for people going through a difficult time.

## Our attorneys

- Amber Calloway, Founding Partner, leads the personal injury practice. She focuses on car accidents and slip and fall (premises liability) matters.
- Desmond Pea, Founding Partner, leads the family law practice: divorce, child custody and child support. He is trained in mediation.
- Priya Natarajan, Associate Attorney, works on workplace injury claims, child support and car accident matters. She speaks English and Tamil.

## How we work

We explain each step in plain language, put fees in writing before work begins, and return calls and emails within one business day. Injury and family matters sometimes overlap, so our attorneys work together when a case needs both.

## Languages

Our team can assist in English and Tamil. If you need another language, tell us when you book and we will try to arrange an interpreter.
