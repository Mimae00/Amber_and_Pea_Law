# Frequently asked questions

<!-- Sample content for a fictional firm. -->

## What kinds of cases do you handle?

We handle personal injury matters (car accidents, slip and fall / premises liability, workplace injuries) and family law matters (divorce, child custody, child support). We do not handle criminal defense, immigration or business law.

## How do I book a consultation?

Use the Book a Consultation page to pick a date and time, call (555) 010-0142 during office hours, or send your details through the contact form and we will reach out within one business day.

## How long does a case take?

It depends on the matter. Some family law matters resolve in weeks, while injury cases can take months or longer. An attorney can discuss realistic timelines for your situation during a consultation, but no timeline or outcome can be guaranteed.

## Can the website assistant give me legal advice?

No. The website assistant shares general information from this website only. It cannot give legal advice, assess your case or predict outcomes. For advice about your situation, book a free consultation with an attorney.

## Is what I tell the chat assistant confidential?

Please don't share confidential or sensitive details in the chat or website forms. Chatting with the assistant does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Our regular hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Limited early or late appointments may be arranged by phone for urgent matters.

## Do I need a lawyer for a minor car accident?

Many people with minor accidents handle claims themselves, while others prefer help when injuries, disputed fault or insurance problems are involved. A free consultation can help you understand your options; the assistant cannot tell you whether you need a lawyer.
