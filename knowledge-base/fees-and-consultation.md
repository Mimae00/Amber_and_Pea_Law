# Fees and free consultation

<!-- Sample content for a fictional firm. -->

## Free initial consultation

Your first consultation is free and has no obligation. It lasts about 30 minutes and can be in person at our office, by phone or by video call. You can book a time online on the Book a Consultation page or by calling (555) 010-0142.

## What to bring to a consultation

Bring anything related to your matter, for example: police or accident reports, photos, medical bills, insurance letters, court papers, existing custody or support orders, and a short timeline of what happened. If you don't have documents yet, come anyway.

## Personal injury fees (car accidents, slip and fall, workplace injuries)

How much does it cost, and what do we charge for an injury case? Personal injury matters, including car accident cases, are typically handled on a contingency fee basis. That means you pay attorney fees only if money is recovered for you, as a percentage agreed in writing before work begins. There is no upfront fee for injury cases. Case costs, such as filing fees or medical record fees, are explained in the written agreement.

## Family law fees (divorce, custody, child support)

What do we charge for a family law case? Family law matters such as divorce, custody and child support are usually billed hourly or, for some simpler matters, at a flat fee. We explain the fee structure in writing before any work begins, and we can give you a cost estimate after the free consultation.

## Payment options

We accept credit and debit cards and bank transfers. Payment plans may be available for family law matters; ask during your consultation.

## Does a consultation make me a client?

No. A consultation, a website form or a chat with the website assistant does not create an attorney-client relationship. That only happens once you sign a written engagement agreement with the firm.
