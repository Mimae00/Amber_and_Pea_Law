## Car accidents

We help people injured in car, truck and motorcycle collisions. Our team reviews police reports, medical records and insurance policies, explains the options in plain language, and handles communication with insurance companies so you can focus on recovery.

## What to do after a car accident

General steps many people take: get medical attention, report the accident to the police, take photos of the scene and vehicles, collect contact details of witnesses and the other drivers, and keep copies of medical bills and repair estimates. Be careful about giving recorded statements to an insurance company before you understand your options.

## Slip and fall and premises liability

Property owners have duties to keep their premises reasonably safe. If someone is hurt because of a hazard such as a wet floor, broken stairs or poor lighting, photos of the hazard, incident reports and witness details can be important. We can explain what information to gather. Learn more on the Slip and Fall page: /practice-areas/slip-and-fall.

## Workplace injuries

Workplace injuries may involve workers' compensation and, in some cases, claims against third parties such as equipment manufacturers or contractors. We help clients understand the process, the paperwork and the timelines. Learn more on the Workplace Injuries page: /practice-areas/workplace-injuries.

## Deadlines

Injury claims are subject to legal deadlines (statutes of limitations) that vary by location and type of claim. Missing a deadline can prevent a claim, so it is best to speak with an attorney early. The website assistant cannot tell you which deadline applies to your situation.
